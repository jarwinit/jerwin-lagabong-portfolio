Nene's Budin

01

A responsive website concept for a Tayabas Budin shop, designed to present its heritage, products and ordering paths with clarity.

Role Web Designer and Front End Developer

Focus Brand expression, content hierarchy and responsive browsing

Status Portfolio project

Design approach

I used rich orange, sunny yellow and warm cream to echo a Filipino fiesta. Editorial serif headlines bring heritage and personality, while rounded cards and strong calls to action keep the experience friendly and easy to navigate.

Thoughtful details

Sunburst patterns, bunting and the illustrated hurno give the website a sense of place. Product photography and simple contact actions keep the focus on the food and make ordering feel personal.

CallFlow Dental

02

An interface design for a customer service workspace that brings call handling, follow ups and booking outcomes into a clear operational flow.

Role UI/UX Designer

Focus Information hierarchy, task visibility and privacy aware interface design

Status Interface design only

Design approach

I created a modern and user friendly interface using a calm teal palette, clear labels and consistent spacing. The information hierarchy helps users scan priorities and status changes quickly during a busy workday.

System thinking

The concept organises task tracking, booking outcomes, attendance, reports and end of day records into one consistent system. Large status cards and focused navigation reduce visual noise and make the next action easier to find.

ABOUT ME

Websites built with clarity, purpose, and craft.

I'm Jerwin Lagabong, a web developer who builds modern, responsive websites that help businesses present their work clearly and professionally. I combine practical front end development with a strong eye for layout, usability and visual detail.

My focus is on creating digital experiences that are easy to use, work well across devices and feel considered from the first interaction to the last. With a background in computer engineering, I approach each project with both structure and creativity.

I care about clear content, intuitive navigation, responsive performance and design that reflects the business behind it. Each project is shaped with a practical approach, attention to detail and a focus on making the experience polished and easy to use.
